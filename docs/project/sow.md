# Statement of Work — Meridian Holdings / Tribe AI

**Strategic AI Partnership — Insurance Services BU**
Effective as of the Engagement Kickoff Date · Total engagement value: $5,000,000 · Term: 6 months

## 1. Purpose

Meridian Holdings intends to become the most operationally efficient third-party administrator in North America, moving its Insurance Services business unit from reactive to predictive claims operations. Tribe AI is engaged to deliver Meridian's first production AI capability in support of that vision. This Statement of Work sets the goals for that work and the framework governing it.

## 2. Goals

The parties will pursue measurable improvement against the following operating goals. Current values reflect Meridian's Insurance Services baseline at kickoff; targets are directional.

| Goal | Current | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Cost-to-serve | $430–$650 per claim across intake channels | Blended −25% |
| Cycle time | 68h average | <24h (simple claims) |
| Review rate | 45% of claims flagged for human review | <20% |
| Customer satisfaction | CSAT 3.2 | >4.0 |

These goals are high-level. Specific initiative scope, milestones, and measurement plans will be defined during the Discovery Period and agreed in writing at its conclusion.

## 3. Structure

**Discovery Period (Weeks 1–2).** A joint Tribe and Meridian team works on the ground to understand claims operations and the data and constraints around them. The period concludes with an agreed initiative scope, documented and signed by both parties, that governs the remainder of the engagement.

**Delivery Period (through end of Month 6).** Tribe builds and validates the agreed capability and moves it toward production, working within Meridian's operational and compliance environment.

**Midpoint Checkpoint (end of Month 3).** The parties conduct a formal review of progress against the agreed scope and goals. Continuation beyond the month-3 checkpoint depends on demonstrated performance against a claims review standard agreed with Meridian Compliance.

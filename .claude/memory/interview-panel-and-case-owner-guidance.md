From a clarification call with the case owner (Trevor Noon, Tribe) before the Monday 4pm session.

**Panel:**

- **Nikolina (PM, met before):** user journey, jobs to be done, change management for users moving from a legacy system to a modern one. Likely opens the session.
- **Charles (designer):** process: the steps taken, iterations, why each design choice.
- **Pooja (technical lead):** "what's the most technically complex part of what you showed?"; unhappy paths; "are the goals realistic?"

**Format:** expect questions throughout, not 30 minutes then 15. The candidate runs the meeting after introductions. Assessed partly on "would we put this person in front of a client?": professional, not overly casual.

**Scoping advice:** go deep on one user and one end-to-end experience rather than broad. Every element must be defensible; "AI made it" is a failure mode. Opinionated assumptions are welcome; expect "what if that assumption is wrong?"

**Topics to cover:** show the process from first principles ("how the sausage is made"). Call out data gaps and odd patterns, some of which are planted. Address the learning loop: how a correction improves the next cycle without per-claim clicking.

**Why:** It sets the bar for the deck and demo (see [deliverable](/deliverable-is-ux-pov-deck-and-demo.md)).

**How to apply:** Pick one adjuster journey and build it deep. Prepare answers for Pooja's three questions. Include a process slide and a data-findings slide. Show the learning loop in the demo, not just describe it.
